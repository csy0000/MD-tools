**Interface, as agreed with S4:** a caller sets a state with `set_state(context, state)` and never
calls `Context.setParameter` on a public name alone, because the charge weights are derived
parameters. `state` names exactly `lambda_electrostatics`, `lambda_sterics` and `lambda_bonded`.
`set_state` refuses a Context that lacks any parameter. `derivatives` always returns all three and
restores the Context's state. Force groups used: 0-10; group 16 is left free for S4's restraint.

## Findings other sessions need

1. **OpenMM 8.6 does not recompute a NonbondedForce's dispersion correction when an offset
   changes** through a global parameter. Anything lambda-dependent in a NonbondedForce's LJ
   silently keeps its full-weight tail.
2. **OpenMM's long-range corrections are not additive over subsets.** NonbondedForce returns
   N/(N+1) x (distinct pairs + self pairs); a grouped CustomNonbondedForce returns exactly N/(N+1)
   of its distinct pairs. Pinned by `test_openmm_long_range_corrections_are_not_additive_over_subsets`.
3. **A custom long-range correction is re-integrated by quadrature on every parameter change**:
   ~5e-8 kJ/mol of non-linearity in lambda, while its derivative is exact. That bounds
   finite-difference checks, not estimators.
4. **OpenMM aborts the process** (`terminate`, not an exception) when a long-range correction's
   energy-parameter derivative simplifies to zero.
5. **pmemd reports the unscaled terms on softcore atoms outside EPtot and outside its MBAR energies**
   (the "Softcore part", SC_EPtot). A cross-engine or an MBAR comparison that ignores this is off
   by a lambda-independent constant (164 kJ/mol here).
