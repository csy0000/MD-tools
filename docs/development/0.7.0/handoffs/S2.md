## Findings other sessions need

1. **Dispersion correction counts dummies (S3, S4).** OpenMM's long-range correction averages
   over every particle, so a zero-epsilon dummy shifts it (-1.2e-4 kJ/mol for one dummy in the
   fixture box; volume-dependent). `endpoint_accounting` names it. A softcore that moves LJ out
   of `NonbondedForce` changes this bookkeeping.
2. **Junction terms are lambda-dependent (S3).** A `dummy-removed` term has force constant 0 at
   its dummy end and full strength at its physical end. Not every bonded term is constant along
   the path.
3. **An OpenMM PDB write/read round trip does not reach a fixed point (S0, S1).** Reading the
   plan's `combined.pdb`, writing it and reading it again reorders the bond between the ligand
   and the appended residue on every pass, and `rest2.selection.topology_digest`, which hashes
   bonds in iteration order, alternates between two values forever (file-only reproduction sent
   to S0). The plan therefore keeps the exact PDB text it read its topology from and writes
   those bytes verbatim, so the one digest authority is stable across write/load cycles
   (`test_a_plan_rewritten_from_a_loaded_plan_is_the_same_plan`). Whether `topology_digest`
   itself should become order-independent is S0's ruling and S1's function.
4. **Private imports (S0), ruled.** `ligands.package._subsystem_table` and `_compare_tables` stay
   imported until integration, where S0 renames them `subsystem_parameter_table` and
   `compare_parameter_tables` and switches this import in the same commit.

### Unique-group internal nonbonded terms (S0 ruling, 2026-09-19)

S3's Hamiltonian kept a unique group's internal nonbonded pairs at full strength at every lambda;
this plan annihilated them. S0 ruled for S3's (Amber) convention, with ONE definition of the
endpoint System: the plan's. Implemented:

- a unique group's own exceptions keep their physical values at its dummy end; its non-excluded
  internal pairs are carried there by a `CustomBondForce` named `UniqueGroupInternalNonbonded`
  (vacuum Coulomb with OpenMM's constant 138.93545764438198, plus Lennard-Jones), present in
  both Systems and zero at the physical end, where the NonbondedForce computes them;
- per connected group only. S3's `ghost()` sums over every unique atom of a side; for two groups
  on different anchors the pairs between them depend on physical coordinates and do not
  separate, so the plan excludes them. For a single group (S3's tail, both fixtures here) the two
  agree. S3 is told;
- `dummy_energy`, `factorization_check` and `endpoint_accounting` name the term
  (`internal_exceptions` in the NonbondedForce class, `internal_pairs` in the CustomBondForce
  class); `audit_plan` allows exactly the group-internal exceptions to be nonzero at a dummy end;
- a fixture that can see it, `tests/data/alchemy/internal-v1/`: n-pentane (AM1-BCC, made with an
  empty temporary `MD_DATA`), whose unmapped propyl has internal 1-4 exceptions and 1-5 pairs.
  v1 is unchanged.

Tests: the internal energy the plan names equals a sum computed by hand from package B's table
(not from the plan); endpoint A in water closes to 1e-7 per force class with it; the same
endpoint test FAILS by more than 100 x the tolerance when the internal terms are annihilated
(`test_annihilating_the_internal_terms_fails_the_endpoint_test`); both pentane endpoints recover
in vacuum; the propyl group still factorizes. Endpoint B of pentane is not tested in the fixture
water box: the superposed propyl overlaps water there, and those energies are too large for a
1e-7 absolute comparison to mean anything.
