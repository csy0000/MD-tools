## Findings other sessions need

1. **Dispersion correction counts dummies (S3, S4).** OpenMM's long-range correction averages
   over every particle, so a zero-epsilon dummy shifts it (-1.2e-4 kJ/mol for one dummy in the
   fixture box; volume-dependent). `endpoint_accounting` names it. A softcore that moves LJ out
   of `NonbondedForce` changes this bookkeeping.
2. **Junction terms are lambda-dependent (S3).** A `dummy-removed` term has force constant 0 at
   its dummy end and full strength at its physical end. Not every bonded term is constant along
   the path.
3. **An OpenMM PDB write/read round trip does not reach a fixed point (S0, S1).** Reading the
   plan's `combined.pdb`, writing it and reading it again reorders the bond between the ligand
   and the appended residue on every pass, and `rest2.selection.topology_digest`, which hashes
   bonds in iteration order, alternates between two values forever (file-only reproduction sent
   to S0). The plan therefore keeps the exact PDB text it read its topology from and writes
   those bytes verbatim, so the one digest authority is stable across write/load cycles
   (`test_a_plan_rewritten_from_a_loaded_plan_is_the_same_plan`). Whether `topology_digest`
   itself should become order-independent is S0's ruling and S1's function.
4. **Private imports (S0), ruled.** `ligands.package._subsystem_table` and `_compare_tables` stay
   imported until integration, where S0 renames them `subsystem_parameter_table` and
   `compare_parameter_tables` and switches this import in the same commit.
