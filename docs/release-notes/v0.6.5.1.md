# 0.6.5.1 — torsional analysis

A release of the torsional-state analysis (`md_tools.analysis`) and nothing else. **No simulation code
is touched**: no run, stage, ladder, scaler or Hamiltonian changes, and no GPU test was run, because
none of them can be affected. Two changes alter results for some inputs, and this note says which.

## 1. `allow_single_cluster` now defaults to False

`t_hdbscan` took `allow_single_cluster=True` from 2026-10-01; it is back to sklearn's default, False.
The reason is measured: on c(RGDfV), the 19-column torsion set (the 17 of the tutorial plus Asp `chi2`
and Arg `chi5`) gave **one** cluster with True and **two** with False, while the 17-column set gave two
under both. Allowing the root of the condensed tree let a multi-state ensemble collapse into one cluster
as soon as a torsion was added.

The cost is the one the opt-in exists for: with False a **unimodal** ensemble cannot come back as one
state and is split (a 4000-frame Gaussian splits into 2). `summary()` records the flag and says so. Pass
`allow_single_cluster=True` for an ensemble that may be one state. Results change only for an ensemble
that the old default reported as one cluster.

## 2. Canonicalization of resonance-equivalent atoms

A graph with localised bond orders and charges draws a carboxylate as `C=O` and `C-O-`, so the two
oxygens were different atoms: no permutation exchanged them, and a torsion measured to one oxygen was never
folded onto the torsion measured to the other, half a turn away. A clustering built on those columns split
one conformer into two. The symptom is two groups that differ only by half a turn in one torsion to a
carboxylate oxygen.

`enumerate_symmetry`, `SymmetricTorsionDescriptor` and `t_hdbscan` now **canonicalise** the molecule
before matching (`canonicalize=True`, the default): charges and bond orders are replaced by the resonance
hybrid's, and the terminal oxygens (or sulfurs) of a sulfur, phosphorus or chlorine centre are one atom by
rule, because RDKit does not generate the alternative drawings. Aromatic atoms and bonds keep their own
values, a structure count at RDKit's limit refuses, and every returned permutation is re-verified.
`canonicalize=False` is the previous behaviour. **`resonance=`**, the name of this option while it was on
`main`, still works and says it is the former name.

| group | automorphisms, drawn → canonicalised |
|---|---|
| acetate | 6 → 12 |
| benzoate | 2 → 4 |
| guanidinium (N-methyl) | 24 → 48 |
| acetamidinium | 24 → 48 |
| nitromethane | 6 → 12 |
| nitrate, carbonate | 2 → 6 |
| methanesulfonate, methyl phosphate | 12 → 36 |
| acetic acid, amides, ureas, phenol, paracetamol, phenolate | unchanged |

c(RGDfV): 36 864 → 147 456 automorphisms, about 14 s to enumerate. Its 17 tutorial torsions are not moved
by the carboxylate and guanidinium exchanges, so their four operations and two clusters are unchanged;
adding Asp `chi2` and Arg `chi5` gives 16 operations and folds each of their two peaks into one. **Results
change only where a selected torsion passes through such a group.** Tautomers are not resonance and are
not handled. The legacy post-hoc `TorsionSymmetry` route still compares the drawn structure.

## 3. A crystal structure assigned to a cluster

* `reference_assignments(structure, molecule, quads)`: one row of torsions per atom assignment of a
  reference structure (a crystal ligand: no hydrogens, bond orders not trusted), by a heavy-atom graph match
  with the stereocentres checked. `NaN` where a torsion goes through a hydrogen.
* `descriptor.nearest_labelling(candidates, reference)`: the assignment nearest a reference row, over the
  columns every candidate can measure, so one structure is one line per cluster, not one per assignment.
* `fit.assign_reference(candidates)`: per cluster, the distance to its density peak, to its nearest frame
  and the fraction of its frames nearer the peak than the reference. **It returns no best cluster when the
  measurable columns cannot separate two clusters** (`best_cluster` is `None`, with `consistent_with` and
  `indistinguishable`).
* Also new: `fit.density_peak`, `fit.align_all(per_cluster=)` and `descriptor.align_to_reference` write every
  frame in the labelling nearest one reference (the Dirichlet-domain rule), with a margin per frame.

**Paracetamol, 4YJI (1.73 A) and 4A9K (1.81 A), ligand `TYL`, from the PDB model server.** Each crystal has
two atom assignments, `aryl` half a turn apart, and each cluster row shows one line. **Neither crystal can be
assigned to cluster A or B**: on the columns a crystal can measure, the two density peaks coincide once the
ring flip is applied, because A and B differ only in the hydroxyl hydrogen. The notebook says so. These are
paracetamol bound to a protein, not the drug's own crystal. Not run on the 4ibi complex, whose torsion table
and structure were not available here.

## 4. Symmetry-corrected RMSD

`symmetric_rmsd`, `intercluster_rmsd`, `operations_on_atoms` and `heavy_atoms`: the minimum over the
symmetry operations of the superposed RMSD, exact (Kabsch for each operation), on the same operations the
torsional distance uses, restricted to the atoms compared. **Heavy atoms only, by decision**: all 79 atoms of
c(RGDfV) would need 147 456 operations, the 41 heavy atoms need 16.

On c(RGDfV) heavy atoms, 20 frames and 380 pairs plus adversarial relabellings, spyrmsd (all graph
isomorphisms) agreed to 0.002 A, and cpptraj `symmrmsd` to 0.004 A. With **polar hydrogens added, cpptraj
gave an RMSD below the minimum over valid operations in 29 of 100 frames, by up to 0.07 A**; it was not used
as a reference. About 2.6 us per pair and operation in numpy; the 4000-frame all-pairs figure (about 5
minutes with 16 operations) is extrapolated, not a run of that size. Paracetamol: the heavy-atom RMSD is
0.32 ± 0.15 A between A and B and within each, so **heavy-atom RMSD does not separate its two clusters**.

## What was checked, and what was not

**Fast lane** (`not slow and not gpu`, CPU only, cores 35-38, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=""`) on `2e9e205f`:
3192 passed, 3 skipped, 5 xfailed, **1 failed**, 638 deselected, in 27 min. The failure was
`test_the_legacy_fit_uses_the_same_multiplicities_as_its_classifier`, which asked a unimodal fixture for one
cluster and so relied on the old `allow_single_cluster=True` default. It was **migrated, not removed**: it now
passes `allow_single_cluster=True`, which is the question it asks. After that edit the six analysis test files
(the new ones and the two existing ones) were re-run: 116 passed. **The full lane was not re-run after that
one-test edit.** Four tests of the default (`test_analysis_t_hdbscan_contract.py`) were rewritten to pin the
new default in both directions.

**Not run, on purpose:** the `gpu or slow` lane and every GPU test. This release touches
`md_tools.analysis` only and no simulation, stage, ladder, scaler or Hamiltonian code, so there is nothing
for them to exercise. **Also not run:** the CUDA coverage inventory guard (it is `gpu`-marked and needs a
card), and the full CI, whose result is a separate fact. The strict documentation build passes.

**Not established:** the clustering on the 4ibi complex that motivated the canonicalization (its structure
and torsion table were not available here); the 4000-frame all-pairs RMSD time (extrapolated); and
cpptraj's undershoot with polar hydrogens beyond the 100 frames tested, whose cause was not traced.
