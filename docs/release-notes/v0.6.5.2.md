# 0.6.5.2 — extension fixes, `kind: peptide` contents, a gauge for drawing torsions

Three unrelated changes. The first two alter behaviour for some inputs and this note says which. **No
Hamiltonian, scaler, integrator or exchange rule is touched.**

## 1. Extending a REST2 ladder: two crashes

Both were reported against real extension chains and reproduced on their manifests before being fixed.

* **A segment shorter than the original request could not be extended again.** Extension-parent
  validation compared the parent's rows to the **original** request's exchange count. The segment's own
  count (`extends.segment.exchanges`) is now the expectation, and it takes precedence over the schedule
  budget and the identity floor (`validate_replica_output(..., expected_exchanges=)`,
  `segment_exchanges`).
* **A v2 extension of a v2 extension with empty chain totals crashed at completion**, after the compute,
  with `int(None)` and no manifest written. Chain totals that are unavailable now stay unavailable down the
  chain, as they already did for a v1 parent. A missing acceptance count used to be turned into 0 and
  added, which would have published the last segment's acceptance as the whole chain's; it is now left
  empty.

A chain whose last segment crashed this way has all its exchanges and no manifest. Whether `--resume`
recovers such a directory was **not** tested; keep it until that is tried.

## 2. `kind: peptide`: the chains and their crystal waters, and nothing else

A `.pdb` or `.cif` for `kind: peptide` may hold protein residues (and caps such as ACE and NME) and
waters. An **ion, a crystallisation additive (DMSO, EDO) or a ligand is refused by name**, exit 2, nothing
written; `kind: complex` builds ligands. **Crystal waters are kept, as before**, and the new
`build-top --strip-water` removes them before the build and records how many. It is refused where it would
do nothing: another kind, a `.seq`/`.smi`/`.sdf` input, `--parameterize`, `--rest2-scaler`.

**This can refuse a file that built before.** Ions are refused because only chains and water were asked
for, so a structure that carries zinc or chloride (the 1TYL assembly) needs them stripped from your file
or `kind: complex`. The barnase-barstar tutorial's assembly 3 is protein plus 90 waters and passes the
check; the full tutorial build was **not** re-run for this release.

## 3. A gauge on one torsion column, for drawing

`align_to_reference` and `THDBSCAN.align_all` take `gauge=(column, width)` or `(column, lo, hi)`. A ring
flip moves two torsions together (paracetamol's `aryl` and `phenol`), so folding each into a half-circle on
its own merges conformers; the gauge fixes **one** column to a window centred on the reference's value and
the others follow it, by the operation that moves them together. Frames on the cut take the closest miss and
are reported (`gauge_satisfied`, `gauge_edge_distance`, `gauge_window`), never silently moved. The clustering
distance is untouched. Explained in
Methods and shown in section 10 of the paracetamol notebook, both since moved into
[Torsion preprocessing](../openmm_methods/analysis/torsional-clustering/torsion-preprocessing.ipynb#a-gauge-on-one-column). It was **not** run on
folic acid, which motivated it: there was no trajectory to run it on.
