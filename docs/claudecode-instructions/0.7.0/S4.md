## Files you own

New alchemical execution, reporting and analysis modules, the cycle tests, and the tutorials for
them. You do not own the topology builder (S2), the Hamiltonian (S3), or the CLI, schemas and data
contract (S0).

## What you are building

Fixed-lambda window execution through the **existing** authorities — preflight, platform policy,
MPI, checkpoints, cost accounting, registration — plus energy and derivative reporting and the
TI / FEP / BAR / MBAR analysis over it.

Start on the frozen energy and derivative fixtures at lambda 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1, so the
estimators and their uncertainty handling are finished and tested before a real Hamiltonian
exists. Then connect S3's.

## What the records must carry

Named state and sample IDs with units; the reduced potentials the ensemble actually requires,
including pressure–volume terms under NPT; sample counts; derivative components; uncertainty and
overlap information.

**Sample provenance is the state a sample came from — never the rank of the worker that processed
it.** The two are equal only by accident, and only until a campaign is resumed on a different
number of GPUs.

TI and FEP are analyses of one Hamiltonian, not two implementations of the potential. If they were
two, their agreement would prove nothing. Use stable exponential averaging for FEP and report
overlap limitations plainly.

## Cycles

Absolute hydration, then relative hydration (vacuum and solvent legs), then relative binding
(solvent and complex legs, consistent orientation and sign conventions), then absolute binding —
with Boresch-style or otherwise validated restraints, the restraint attachment and release
contributions, and the **standard-state correction**. A binding free energy missing that term is a
different quantity wearing the same name.

## Execution rules you inherit and must not re-implement

Nothing is written until the whole preflight passes. A refused continuation is read-only,
including its own logs. A checkpoint commit is a generation transaction through
`md_tools.openmm.checkpoint`. CUDA selection is truthful and there is no silent fall back. Exchange
between windows, if added at all, goes through the existing MPI authority — a second
`except ImportError: return` is a second policy, and it will be the one that runs.

Alchemical NPT needs ensemble-correct state energies and checkpoints. REST2's NVT rule belongs to
REST2; do not impose it here.

## Before any long campaign

Write the acceptance matrix: fixture, expected quantity, independent reference, tolerance,
platform, command, evidence path. Thresholds are fixed **before** sampling. The agreement gate for
matched simple references is below both 0.5 kcal/mol and three combined standard errors, including
reference and integration uncertainty, with a justified floor for effectively exact fixtures.
Inconclusive uncertainty is not a pass, and a tolerance is never weakened after a failure.

A 10 ns trajectory and a green unit-test lane are not proof that a free-energy method is correct.

## Working rules

Small commits on your own branch, with real evidence in your handoff. Validate sample identity,
units, chain-rule derivatives, uncertainty, resume, exports and registration. Do not merge, do not
release, do not edit another session's worktree.
