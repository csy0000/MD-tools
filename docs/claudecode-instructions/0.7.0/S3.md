## What you are building

The Amber18 softcore Hamiltonian, `sc: true` by default, `scalpha = 0.5`, `scbeta = 12` Å²
(0.12 nm² internally). Amber18 manual section 21.1.5, equations 21.5–21.7, with R4 as the
implementation reference. Define the appearing and disappearing directions explicitly, and every
intramolecular and exception rule.

Work against the agreed miniature topology-plan fixtures while S2 builds the real builders. Do not
wait for S2, and do not write your own topology builder to avoid waiting.

## The four ways this goes wrong quietly

1. **Mislabelling the potential.** OpenFE's default Gapsys potential, and its Beutler LJ option,
   are not Amber18 equivalence. Selecting one and shipping it as `amber18` puts a false label on a
   validated-looking number. A later AMBER smoothstep variant is likewise a different function.
2. **Half-softened electrostatics.** Softening a real-space Coulomb term while its original
   contribution survives in reciprocal space, in the self or background terms, or in an exclusion,
   gives smooth, plausible, wrong free energies. Validate the full PME decomposition.
3. **Incomplete derivatives.** OpenMM custom-force derivatives alone do not prove coverage of
   `NonbondedForce` parameter offsets. `dU/ds` must include softcore, electrostatics and PME,
   exceptions, bonded, restraint and applicable long-range correction terms.
4. **A background that hides the defect.** A solvent background of tens of thousands of kJ/mol
   will swallow a missing term. Report per-component errors, not only totals.

**Do not carry over `dU/dlambda = U1 - U0`.** It is exact for AIS because that path is linear. A
softcore path is not, and reusing the identity silently produces wrong derivatives — the finite
difference stays the definition of what a derivative check compares against.

Keep the softcore functional form separate from the lambda schedule. Record whether electrostatics
and sterics change together or in stages; validate the chosen schedule and explain it.
`sc: false` selects a documented ordinary path for cases that support it and refuses unsafe
particle insertion or removal rather than re-enabling softcore behind the user's back.
