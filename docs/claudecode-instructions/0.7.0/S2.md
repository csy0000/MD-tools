## Files you own

New topology-building modules (suggested `src/md_tools/alchemy/topology.py` and siblings — agree
the package name with S0 before the first commit), their tests, and their method documentation.

Reuse `md_tools.ligands` **through its existing API**: `LigandPackage`, `resolve_package`,

## What you are building

A topology plan: a record, independent of the MD runner, that `combine-topology` writes and that
the Hamiltonian builder, the executor and the analysis all read. Section 4 of the shared contracts
lists what it contains; the construction checks are the work.

Start from the miniature endpoint pair in the shared fixtures — neutral, one atom substituted,
small enough to check both directions of the map by hand.

## Three things that are easy to get wrong

1. **There is no A→B atom map in this repository today.** `heavy_atom_map` maps deposited atom
   names to package atom names within one compound; `ais.two_state` refuses any pair whose
   particles differ, which is an identity map. You are writing something new, not extending either.
2. **One matched environment.** Two independently solvated endpoint boxes are not atom-matched.
   Treating them as if they were produces a plan that builds, runs, and is wrong.
3. **Raw endpoint energy comparison is not an endpoint test.** Dummy partition-function
   contributions, retained bonded terms and restraint effects must cancel by a demonstrated
   construction or be corrected explicitly. A comparison that ignores them can pass while the
   construction is broken.

Parameter values and chemical states are immutable. A standard OpenFE workflow must never silently
reparameterize a registered MD-tools ligand — if the parameters that ran are not the parameters the
package records, the result is not comparable to anything else in the catalog, which is the entire
point of the catalog.

Follow OpenFE's construction approach (R6, reviewed commit `446961f654e2f2ba9145d5b8de1ce90ef41122ff`,
`src/openfe/protocols/openmm_rfe/_rfe_utils/relative.py` and its tests). Its documented dummy-group
limitation must be **addressed** by your construction, not inherited. Separated topology is
deferred: do not implement it and do not advertise it.

## First task

The callable builder and its provenance record, against the miniature fixtures, with:
mapping symmetry in both directions; chemical ambiguity detected; force-field compatibility;
missing, duplicated and omitted terms; endpoint recovery; and every source hashed.

The `combine-topology` CLI surface is S0's — write the callable layer, and S0 wires it into
`md-openmm`. It stays a subcommand of the one executable.
